### Pé na Areia
- Definition：Internal product name of the sustainable delivery app for the Santos waterfront; serves as an iFood-branded entry card within the iFood prototype.
- Aliases：pe-na-areia

### postos de referência
- Definition：Official reference points (saved spots) on the Santos waterfront used instead of formal street addresses for delivery location selection; seeded automatically with four Santos locations on first run.
- Aliases：reference points、ReferencePoint

### pin de localização
- Definition：A customer-placed map pin (GeoJSON coordinate pair) that pins the delivery destination; stored in `Order.pinCliente` and intended to be locked after payment to replicate Domino's Pinpoint Delivery behavior.
- Aliases：pinCliente、delivery pin

### quiósque
- Definition：Partner kiosks/restaurants listed through the `/kiosks` endpoint; the customer-facing ordering surface where menus and cart live.
- Aliases：kiosk、Kiosk

### entregador
- Definition：Courier/delivery agent role whose screens (orders list, route map, delivery confirmation, chat) are still marked as pending implementation.
- Aliases：courier、Courier
